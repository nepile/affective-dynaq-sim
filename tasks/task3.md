### Penjabaran metric evaluasi pembelajaran agent

Ada 2 fase yaitu fase awal dan fase stabil dengan pembagian 80/20 (80% fase awal dan 20% fase stabil). berarti nanti di konfigurasi harus ada properti yang mengatur persentase fase awal dan fase stabil.

#### Hitung metrik di bawah ini untuk Fase Awal (misal ada 200 episode, berarti untuk fase awal adalah 160 episode)
1. Steps per episodes: Number of steps until the goal is reached
2. Current Cumulative Reward (CCR): Recording the cumulative total reward obtained by the agent as the number of episodes increases throughout the learning process
3. Current Episodic Reward (CER): Recording the total reward generated by the agent in each individual episode (calculated at the final step of each episode)
4. Convergence Speed: The agent's speed reaches a stable level of performance

#### Hitung metrik di bawah ini untuk Fase Stabil (misal ada 200 episode, berarti untuk fase stabil adalah 40 episode terakhir)
5. Collision Rate: Frequency of collisions or attempted movements toward an obstacle
6. Coefficient of Variation (CV): The Coefficient of Variation (CV) is used to measure the variability of data distribution relative to the mean value, CERavg.
7. Relative Margin Error (RME): Measuring the proportionality of the margin of error relative to the mean value (CERavg), and classifying the level of stability as a performance tolerance
8. Distribution Normal (Gaussian) based on CER avg, variances, confidence interval, and rme.

Untuk nomor 6 dan 7, saya melampirkan persamaan rumusnya dalam pdf. Untuk nomor 8 tinggal ambil datanya dari CER baik itu mean/avg, variance, confidence interval dan RME.
